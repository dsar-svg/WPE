# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Customers** — people craving Chinese food who browse the menu on their phone, select a location, build a cart, and send their order via WhatsApp. They want speed, clarity, and appetite appeal. Primary device: mobile.
- **Admins** — restaurant owners and location managers who log into the admin panel to manage menu items, categories, locations, orders, pricing, and settings. They want efficiency, real-time control, and minimal training. Primary device: desktop.

## Product Purpose

A progressive web app that replaces paper menus and phone-in orders for W Panda Express. Customers view the digital menu, self-serve their cart, and send structured orders via WhatsApp with zero commission fees. Admins update products, prices, stock, and settings in real time across all locations. Success means more orders with less friction — no app store download, no third-party delivery tax.

## Brand Personality

Bold, Warm, Energetic

The brand is unapologetically vibrant — confident reds and warm golds that say "Chinese takeout done right." It feels like walking into a busy, welcoming restaurant where the food comes fast and the flavors hit hard. Friendly without being cutesy, loud without being harsh. The digital presence should translate that same immediacy and appetite energy.

## Anti-references

- Generic fast-food templates — stock-photo hero banners, beige-and-green "fresh" palettes, clip-art icons, corporate sterile layouts
- Over-designed trendy apps — over-animated minimalist-brutalist UIs, glassmorphism decor, gradient text, hand-drawn SVG illustrations, SaaS-card-grid monotony
- Anything that makes ordering feel like a chore rather than a reflex

## Design Principles

1. **Appetite first** — Every screen should make the food look and feel accessible. Large food imagery, clear pricing, zero clicks between "I want this" and "it's in my cart."
2. **Friction is the enemy** — The ordering flow is a straight line: pick a location → pick food → checkout → WhatsApp. No dead ends, no surprise fields, no unnecessary steps.
3. **Mobile-native confidence** — Designed for thumbs first. Touch targets are generous, the cart is a bottom sheet, the menu scrolls like a feed. Desktop follows.
4. **Authentic vibrancy** — The brand's red-gold energy is carried by color and typography, not decoration. No gradients-for-flash, no AI decoration. The confidence comes from the material, not the effects.
5. **Admin power without complexity** — The admin panel packs CRUD across 5+ entities but never feels like a cockpit. Smart defaults, clear labels, instant saves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text contrast ≥4.5:1. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible with visible focus indicators. Touch targets ≥44px. Form inputs have proper labels and error messages. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
